Kindness with care

A feel-good place needs honest boundaries.

Natural-sounding AI can still be mistaken for a human. We’ll make its identity visible and provide controls that support users’ wellbeing.

AI is always labeledClear AI labels in chat and voice/video sessions; no claims that a companion is a human spouse or professional.
Original, licensed mediaFictional avatars and licensed synthetic voices—no unauthorized voice or likeness cloning.
Adults onlyMembership and dating features are intended for people 18 and older.
Privacy and controlExplain data use, provide mute/leave controls, and let people manage or delete their data.
